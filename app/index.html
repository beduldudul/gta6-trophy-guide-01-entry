    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone. One playthrough is enough: there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats should switch off trophies and saving.

      Save discipline (read this first)

      • Chapter saves: one manual save at the start of Chapters 1–4, plus one before the final mission. The game has 15 manual slots plus one autosave — rotate the rest.
      • Chapter 1: do Lucia’s balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends (see Positive Mental Attitude). Keep that save until the trophy pops.
      • Chapter 3: race Yago’s time-sensitive street race the moment it appears (hourglass icon); it feeds Strangers & Freaks.
      • After the story: everything outside the story stays open, and the 100% checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Work done earlier still counts.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is reported missable. Every other trophy stays earnable after the story. The list below is built from the official descriptions and launch-week reporting; where a method is not yet confirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing.

        Almost-missable: watch these

        • Strangers & Freaks — the Chapter 3 race against Yago is time-sensitive. Save first, race at once.
        • Let’s Go Bowling — if you leave a valuable behind at Kingpin Bowling, the operation comes back later, so it is not missable.
        • Story mission trophies — unmissable, but there is no mission replay, so chapter saves are your only safety net.
